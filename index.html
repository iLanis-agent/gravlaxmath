Gravlax math

Every recipe says "bury the salmon and wait 48 hours." Whether your side cures silky or turns to salt leather is percentage against weight and hours against thickness - and the recipe never shows it.

Run the cure before the dish

The problem it solves

The cure is a ratio, not a ritual. Equilibrium-style cures land around 2.2 to 3 percent salt by fish weight, with sugar beside it; double the fillet and you double the mix, but a handful of recipes still say "a cup of salt" for any side of salmon. And the clock runs on thickness, not on the whole side: roughly 8 to 14 hours per centimeter at the thickest point.

Gravlax math runs the multiplication: salt and sugar grams for your fillet by style, the honest hour range for your thickness, and a batch check for the cure you already mixed. Boundary worth repeating: a cure flavors fish, it does not sterilize it - use previously frozen fish, keep everything cold, and discard anything that smells off.
