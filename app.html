Every gravlax recipe says "bury it and wait 48 hours" and skips the arithmetic. Salt percentage against fish weight and hours against thickness decide the cure. Run yours before the salmon hits the dish.

Raw fish is the safety line - read this first. A salt cure flavors fish; it does not sterilize it. Use fish that was commercially frozen or previously frozen to parasite-kill guidance (labeled published norms: 7 days at -20C or colder for home freezers), cure and store at fridge temperature, and discard anything that smells off or feels slimy. When in doubt, throw it out. Pregnant, elderly or immunocompromised guests: skip raw-cured fish entirely.
